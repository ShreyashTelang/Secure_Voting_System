What RSA Provides in SecureVote

Confidentiality and Key Protection: RSA ensures that ballots and symmetric session keys cannot be read by an adversary who intercepts network packets. Because factorization of large integers is computationally intractable without knowing the prime factors p and q, only the election server with private exponent d can recover the plaintext vote.

Crucial Boundary: RSA does not provide integrity or tamper detection on its own. An adversary could alter bits in the ciphertext or inject forged votes without needing to decrypt the payload. To solve this, SecureVote pairs RSA with HMAC-SHA256.
