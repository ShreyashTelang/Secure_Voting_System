How the Vote is Protected

Hybrid Cryptographic Relationship between RSA and HMAC-SHA256
Hybrid Model

SecureVote maintains a clear separation of cryptographic responsibilities. RSA does not generate the HMAC tag, nor does HMAC encrypt the ballot:

RSA Responsibilities:
  • Asymmetric public/private keypair separation.
  • Protects the confidentiality of the voter ballot choice.
  • Protects symmetric session key material in transit.
  • Requires private exponent d to decrypt.
HMAC-SHA256 Responsibilities:
  • RFC 2104 keyed-hashing with SHA-256.
  • Protects transmission packet against tampering.
  • Authenticates possession of the voter's credential secret.
  • Verified using constant-time comparison (compare_digest).
